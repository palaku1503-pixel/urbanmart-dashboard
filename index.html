Key Business Insights

Interpretation and recommendations

1. Revenue Performance
September recorded the highest revenue, while April recorded the lowest revenue.
2. Product Performance
Electronics is the highest revenue-generating category, contributing ₹42,15,977.10.
3. Customer Retention
411 transactions were marked as repeat purchases. Premium customers have the highest repeat-purchase rate.
4. Product Returns
Electronics has the highest return rate at 10.61%. UrbanMart should investigate product quality and return reasons.
5. Delivery and Satisfaction
Delivery time and customer rating have a negative correlation of -0.669. Longer delivery times tend to be associated with lower ratings.
6. Business Recommendations
Improve delivery tracking, introduce customer loyalty rewards and strengthen product quality checks.
